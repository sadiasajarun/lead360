Project Context & Objectives

The opportunity & what success looks like

FIFA Partner Co is activating a global sponsorship. The microsite is the digital front door — it must inform, excite, and convert, across every target market.

  • Drive measurable qualified-lead volume during the campaign window.
  • Deliver a premium brand experience worthy of a global event.
  • Sustain sub-2.5s load times under peak concurrent traffic.
  • Give marketing a self-serve CMS for rapid content updates.
Why it matters

A campaign lives or dies in its first hours. Every second of latency and every friction point in the capture flow is lost pipeline. We engineer for the spike, not the average.

Proposed Technical Approach & Methodology

How we build — deliberately, transparently

A headless, component-driven architecture delivered through short, review-gated iterations so you see progress every week and steer as we go.

We separate presentation from content (headless CMS + statically-generated frontend) so marketing moves fast without engineering bottlenecks, and the site stays instant everywhere.

Delivery Methodology

1

Discover & Design

Workshops, IA, wireframes and a locked visual system.

2

Iterative Build

Weekly increments with staging demos and your sign-off.

3

Harden & Launch

Load testing, QA, and a monitored zero-downtime go-live.

Environments & Governance

Dev → Staging → Prod

Three isolated environments; nothing reaches production unreviewed.

Access & roles

Least-privilege CMS roles; audit trail on every publish.

Weekly demos

Live staging walkthrough each week — decisions logged.
