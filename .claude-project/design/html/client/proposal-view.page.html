AP Invoice Automation with SAP Integration

An intelligent, self-improving accounts-payable pipeline that reads, validates and posts supplier invoices straight into SAP — cutting manual effort and exceptions.
Prepared forATEC Group
EngagementGlobal · Fixed Milestones
Timeline~12 weeks
Date01 Jul 2026
01Executive Summary

From inbox to SAP, automatically

ATEC Group processes thousands of supplier invoices across entities every month. Today that flow is manual — keying data, chasing POs and GRNs, and reconciling exceptions. We propose an AI-driven pipeline that ingests invoices from any channel, extracts and validates line items, matches them against SAP POs/GRNs, and posts clean documents automatically — with a human-in-the-loop only for genuine exceptions.

Outcome

Up to 80% touchless posting, faster cycle time, and a continuously improving accuracy model.

Approach

Proven document-AI + a robust SAP integration layer, delivered in fixed, testable milestones.

07Development Methodology

Fixed milestones, weekly demos

Agile delivery in two-week sprints, grouped into three fixed, payable milestones. You see working software every week, with a shared board for visibility and sign-off at each milestone. Quality is built in: automated unit and integration tests, a labelled invoice test-set to measure extraction accuracy, and a full UAT cycle against the SAP sandbox with sign-off criteria agreed up front.
