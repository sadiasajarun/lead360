1What we're proposing

We're offering ATEC Group an AI-driven Accounts Payable automation system that reads incoming supplier invoices, pulls out the important figures on its own, checks them against purchase orders and goods receipts, and posts the clean results straight into SAP. The finance team stops re-keying invoices by hand and instead reviews only the exceptions the system flags. It's delivered in three milestones so value lands early rather than at the very end.

2Why it matters to this client

ATEC's AP team is drowning in manual entry, and month-end close slips because of it. This directly attacks their two biggest pains: slow invoice throughput and error-prone three-way matching. Faster, auditable posting means a cleaner close and fewer supplier disputes.

3Key highlights

  • Straight-through posting for the majority of standard invoices.
  • Confidence-scored extraction with a human-review queue for anything uncertain.
  • Enforced three-way match before anything reaches SAP.
  • Idempotent SAP adapter with full audit trail and rollback.
  • Phased in three milestones — value from milestone one.

4Talking points

  • "Your team keys thousands of invoices a month — we hand most of that back to them."
  • "Nothing posts to SAP until it passes a three-way match, so the audit trail stays clean."
  • "The model tells you how confident it is; low-confidence items go to a review queue, not into SAP."
  • "You'll see results in milestone one — we don't ask you to wait for a big-bang go-live."
  • "This is the same document-AI approach we ran for HR AI Automation's document + approval workflow."
